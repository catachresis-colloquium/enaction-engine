== Still AGPL-3.0-or-later, and why

The generated ABI pipeline. Its outputs embed the header text emitted by
`src/interface/Abi/Generate.idr`, and `SOURCE.sha256` pins the hashes of its
sources. Changing either side needs the generator re-run (Idris2 and Zig), which
was not run in this change.

* `abi.ipkg`
* `src/interface/Abi/Layout.idr`, `Accelerator.idr`, `Generate.idr`
* `src/interface/generated/abi/enaction_accelerator.{h,jl,rs,zig}`
* `src/interface/generated/abi/SYMBOLS`, `SOURCE.sha256`

Follow-up: change the four emitted header strings in `Generate.idr` to
`MPL-2.0`, regenerate with `scripts/generate-accelerator-abi.sh`, update the
headers of the generator sources, and then remove `LICENSES/AGPL-3.0-or-later.txt`
and the AGPL stanza in `.machine_readable/compliance/reuse/dep5`.
