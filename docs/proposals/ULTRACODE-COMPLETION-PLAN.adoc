= Fastest route to a game-design instrument: ultracode completion plan
:toc:
:toclevels: 2
:sectnums:

Status: **Proposed** (2026-11-04). Architecture:
link:../architecture/GAME-DESIGN-SYSTEM.adoc[GAME-DESIGN-SYSTEM]. Brief for
the parallel AffineScript session:
link:AFFINESCRIPT-SESSION-BRIEF.adoc[AFFINESCRIPT-SESSION-BRIEF].

This plan was produced by an ultracode run. Twenty-two agents mapped 21
estate repositories plus the two proving-ground games. Three independent
architecture proposals followed (elegance-first, dependability-first,
designer-value-first). Three judges scored them, eight adversarial checks
tested the factual claims they rested on, and one synthesis merged them. Every
measured result below was re-run in this session; see the appendix.

== The two fastest routes, in one view

*Engineering critical path.* The first thing a designer can use is the
IDApTIK cognition preview at the end of Wave 4:

. Wave 1: holes first.
** IDApTIK package-runner.
** Observer fix.
** Mandatory digest.
** Lawful ledger.
. Wave 2: `enaction-validate` process seam and `conformance/time/v1`.
. Wave 3: IDApTIK `--emit-epistemic` and the UMS `ProcessPreviewAdapter`.
. Wave 4: explain lens and `just demo-designer`.

That is four waves and about 32 agent-sessions, most of them parallel.
Wave 5 replaces the game's own reasoner with the kernel's and proves Slavia
parity. It is hard-gated on an owner-recorded ADR-0017 T1 want.

*Proof critical path.* Each rung is chosen because a designer-facing label
depends on it:

. Rung 0, gate honesty: no vacuous pass.
. Rung 3, observer noninterference: the fog-of-war guarantee.
. Rung 2, `Certain` is not `Known`.
. Rung 1, status order.
. Rung 4, ledger laws: mass conserved; non-associativity recorded as a
  countermodel.
. Rung 5, generic verdict soundness, upstream.
. Rung 7, Creusot in CI.
. Rung 9, Rocq recheck.

Rungs 6 and 8 (replay and locality, model-to-code correspondence) bind the
Wave 5 reducer.

Fastest is not the same as smallest. The smallest thing that "runs" is a stub
adapter returning canned beliefs, and that is refused: it would be a silent
green and would show designers omniscient agents (see the observer leak).

=== Toolchains

Present in agent containers: cargo and rustc 1.97, bun 1.4.4, Agda 2.6.3
with agda-stdlib 2.3 (installed this session), Coq 8.18, just.

Absent: Idris2, Zig 0.14/0.16, cargo-creusot 0.13 with its pinned nightly,
Why3 and provers, Lean, Nickel, gnatprove, wasmtime, and the aarch64 and
wasm32 Rust targets.

Every gate on an absent tool FAILs locally and is reported CI-only or
ASSUMED. A SessionStart provisioning script would let them run instead; the
precedent is echo-types `scripts/provision-agda.sh`. It is a cross-repo item.

Environment variables used in the gates below: `ENACTION_ROOT`, `UMS_ROOT`,
`IDAPTIK_ROOT` and `SLAVIA_ROOT`, each pointing at a checkout.

== Waves

Each step's `done_when` is a command whose failure is visible. Ratings are
elegance/correctness first, then dependability/security. Steps in a wave run
as parallel agents on disjoint files. One coordinator owns the shared files
(`Cargo.toml`, `Cargo.lock`, `Justfile`, workflows, MANIFESTs), following
`GAME-ENGINE-TEMPLATE-AGENT-PROMPTS.adoc`.

| 11 | Contract evolution + `enaction-validate` (after Q1 and ADR acceptance)
| `FrameDecl` (≤ 64), `FrameTooLarge`, `UnknownHypothesis`. Hypotheses are
  identified by a qualified pair (frame id, hypothesis id), resolved once to
  (frame, bit); bare names repeat across frames (Ghost Lobby uses `unknown`
  in both). Frame declarations are unambiguous: every frame is nonempty,
  frame ids are unique, and hypothesis ids are unique within a frame, refused
  as `EmptyFrame`, `DuplicateFrame` and `DuplicateHypothesis`. Each
  `EpistemicEvent` gains an optional checked evidence payload, a BPA over
  focal masks of its frame, so a reducer can absorb multi-focal and
  categorical evidence; the scalar `confidence` alone cannot carry it. When
  the payload is present it is the one authority: the scalar must equal the
  payload's belief in the event's proposition (the sum of masses of focal
  sets inside it), else `ConfidenceDisagreesWithPayload`, so a report and the
  replay cannot read different evidence from one valid package. The payload
  also carries `source` and `independence_group` (the ledger design's
  mechanism against double counting): a second payload from the same group
  is refused with `DependentEvidence` unless the profile's policy names a
  combination rule for that group.

  Define the versioned runner-output envelope `enaction-runner-output/1`:
  `{package, world_truth: [atom], ledger_trace?}`. World truth cannot travel

  otherwise choose thresholds that legitimise its own beliefs. `ProfileRef`

  the validator refuses with `PolicyDigestMismatch` unless the file's
  canonical digest equals `ProfileRef.policy_digest`.

  those policies, check every truth atom names a declared qualified hypothesis,
  reject a second truth atom for an exclusive frame, pass the envelope
  through with canonical faults; exit 0/1/2.

  §8 open problem 1), which stays the normative authority; no wire change
  lands before the kernel document says it.

== Tropes, types and logics worth pursuing

* **Categorical evidence** as the bridge between relational answers and
  Dempster-Shafer mass. This resolves CAC-KERNEL §8 open problem 1, and it is
  the most "final" result here.
* **The ledger as a Mealy machine** over a checked evidence alphabet, not a
  monoid. Research rung: exact accumulation over `T^k`, with quantisation
  only at observation, gives a genuine monoid action that makes parallel
  merge legal.
* **Echo residue** for every forgetting step: floor division, quantisation,
  and UMS compilation. Each records what it forgets, so diagnostics can cite
  it.
* **The baseline as Stalnaker common ground**, which generalises to per-agent
  common ground through a visibility lattice (Q2 arm B).
* **Factivity split.** A preview is the one place where a world-truth oracle
  exists, so "how wrong are the agents" becomes a designer diagnostic.
* **Tropical MinPlus budgets** for solver fuel and ToM depth. A secret-types
  caution: fuel exhaustion is an observable channel, so report it as an
  explicit verdict.
* **Choreographies** for "an NPC must not believe its message landed without
  an acknowledgement". This waits on μ-recursion in choreographic-types.
* **Typed mod boundary**: overlay restore, the reversible `dlc/vm`, and a
  fail-closed typed-wasm `VerifiedModule`. A hermetic wasm preview is the
  elegant long-term successor to the process seam.
